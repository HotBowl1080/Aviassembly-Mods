# Aviassembly Career missions and rewards

Stock Steam build **26689425**, world **LocalizedVersion**. The game initialized **46 authored missions** at **27 of 30 locations**. This includes **29 multi-delivery missions** with **71 delivery legs**.

Coin payouts are shown as **Relaxed / Normal / Hard**. Regular scrap is shown as **Relaxed and Normal / Hard**. Advanced scrap is the same across all three. For a multi-delivery mission, the bold mission row totals the delivery payments and the completion reward. The indented delivery rows show how that total is paid. Suppliers are the closest sources selected by the game. Actual delivery payouts on an existing save can differ if a job is partially complete or was initialized with different difficulty or world settings.

## Repeatable jobs and other payouts

- After the ending cutscene, eligible non-base locations can generate another contract once their authored chain is complete. These jobs have two or three randomly selected delivery legs. Two-leg contracts award **10 scrap**. Three-leg contracts award **15 scrap and 5 advanced scrap**. Each leg pays coins based on cargo, quantity, and destination pricing, so locations have no fixed repeatable coin payout.
- World scrap pickups give **5 regular scrap** each. Firefighting gives **25 coins per extinguished fire**. These awards are separate from the airport contracts.
- The BackCountry Operations board at Base currently lists **Inland Flyover, Valley Recon, Coastal Patrol, and Rough-Strip Survey**. It records completion and elapsed time. Its current completion code has no coin, scrap, or blueprint payment.

## Source

Activities and payouts were exported from the game’s initialized Career quest definitions in an isolated copy of the installed build. The export followed each airport’s first quest through every follow-up and each delivery inside multi-delivery contracts, and called the game’s mission creation code at all three difficulties. The Normal rewards matched the quests created when the game loaded the world.
