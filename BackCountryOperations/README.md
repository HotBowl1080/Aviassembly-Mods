# BackCountry Operations 0.30.0

[Download BackCountryOperations.dll](https://github.com/HotBowl1080/Aviassembly-Mods/raw/refs/heads/main/BackCountryOperations/BackCountryOperations.dll) · [SHA256](SHA256.txt)

The Hospital and The Wind Turbines use the same **Runway Patch Kit**, available in **Load Cargo** at Base after the earthquake. Each repair takes one kit and each kit uses 10 cargo spaces. At The Wind Turbines, park at the gravel SERVICE stand. At the Hospital, restore power with the two Small Batteries, then park with a patch kit aboard.

Select **Track** on the Hospital or Wind Turbines repair card. Saving and reloading keeps that choice. Before pickup the waypoint points to Base. With a kit aboard it points to the selected airfield. Removing the kit sends the same contract back to Base for pickup.

Only those two runways require patching. The remaining airfields stay in service, with optional relief flights, runway surface upgrades, and lighting projects. Displaced slabs at the damaged strips load individual wheels during touchdown and rollout.

## New in 0.30.0

Valley Supply Trial and Loaded Short-Field Run pay 60 and 100 coins and unlock Utility and High-Lift flaps. Carry two repair kits to complete both runway repairs in one trip. Forest Island map cards show runway dimensions, headings and load margins. Grass grip and runway load limits apply inside designated runway boundaries. Farm Gear Fit Check unlocks upgraded brakes, and the six regional relief flights pay 300 coins in total.

## Installation

1. Close Aviassembly and make sure BepInEx 5 is installed.
2. Copy `BackCountryOperations.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`, replacing any previous BackCountry Operations DLL. Keep one copy of the plugin.
3. Start the game and check that BepInEx reports BackCountry Operations **0.30.0**.

The earlier [v0.75-MaybeAndrewWillAndrew build](https://github.com/HotBowl1080/Aviassembly-Mods/raw/refs/heads/main/BackCountryOperations/BackCountry%20Operations%20v0.75-MaybeAndrewWillAndrew.dll) is available. It identifies itself in BepInEx as 0.27.0.
