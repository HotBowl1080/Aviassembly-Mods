# Aviassembly Atmosphere Weather Edition 1.0.16

[Download Aviassembly.AtmosphereWeather.dll](https://github.com/HotBowl1080/Aviassembly-Mods/raw/refs/heads/main/AtmosphereWeather/Aviassembly.AtmosphereWeather.dll) · [SHA256](SHA256.txt)

Weather Edition adds dynamic weather, wind and gusts, wet runway grip, a day and night cycle, and a precise altitude readout tied to the stock tape. Airport lighting includes runway edge and threshold lights and PAPI, and windsocks follow the local wind. Marginal VFR is the lowest visibility preset, with visibility set to 1.4 to 1.7 statute miles and terrain fading fully by about 2.5 to 3 km at standard weather intensity.

## Install

1. Close Aviassembly and make sure BepInEx 5 is installed and initialized.
2. Back up any installed Weather Edition or full Atmosphere DLL and move it outside `BepInEx/plugins/`. Run only one Atmosphere edition at a time.
3. Copy `Aviassembly.AtmosphereWeather.dll` to `BepInEx/plugins/Aviassembly.AtmosphereWeather/` and restart Aviassembly.
4. Press **O** or click the weather button to open the settings.

## Changes in 1.0.16

The **Airport lights** option in the weather graphics section switches the mod's airport lights between **Shown** and **Hidden** across all islands. **Shown** is the default. Press **Apply** to save or **Cancel** to discard the change. This setting controls runway fixtures, PAPI, and airport lighting. The aircraft stays visible in either mode.
